Our store runs on **Shopify** (hosting, checkout, payment processing, email

terms. Payment providers process your payment data under their own

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and
object to processing based on legitimate interest; where processing is based

You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch supervisory authority, the
Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
