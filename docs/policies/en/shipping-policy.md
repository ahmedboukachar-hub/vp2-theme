# Shipping

## Dispatch

Orders are dispatched from the Netherlands (**Heemstede**). Each piece is carefully packed.

## Processing time

We dispatch your order as quickly as possible after payment has been received.

## Destinations and carriers

We ship to the countries offered at checkout. Shipping is free of charge.

## Rates

The available shipping options and their costs for your address are calculated and shown at checkout before you pay.

## Tracking

Every shipment includes tracking; you receive the tracking link once the order ships.

## Delivery estimates

Delivery times shown by us or the carrier are estimates, not guaranteed dates.

## Customs and import charges (non-EU destinations)

For destinations outside the EU, import duties and taxes may be charged by the destination country on delivery. Unless stated otherwise at checkout, these are payable by the recipient.

## Lost or damaged deliveries

If a delivery appears lost, or arrives damaged, contact us promptly via the contact page with your order number (and photos of any damage). We will take it up with the carrier and ensure the statutory position is honoured — the risk of loss or damage in transit rests with us until the item has been delivered to you.

## Address errors

Please check your delivery address at checkout. If a parcel is returned to us because the address provided was incorrect, we will contact you about redelivery; additional shipping costs for redelivery may apply.
