## Lost or damaged deliveries

If a delivery appears lost, or arrives damaged, contact us promptly via the
contact page with your order number (and photos of any damage). We will take
it up with the carrier and ensure the statutory position is honoured — the risk
of loss or damage in transit rests with us until the item has been delivered
to you.

because the address provided was incorrect, we will contact you about
redelivery; additional shipping costs for redelivery may apply.
