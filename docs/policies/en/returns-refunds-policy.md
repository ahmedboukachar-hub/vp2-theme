# Refund policy

## 1. Right of withdrawal (change of mind)

As a consumer buying at a distance you may withdraw from your purchase within **14 days** of receiving the item, without giving a reason.

- **How to notify:** inform us unambiguously within those 14 days — for example by email via the contact page. You may use the model withdrawal form but are not required to.
- **Return deadline:** send the item back within 14 days of notifying us.
- **Return address:** **Joke Smitlaan 44, 2104 TG Heemstede, the Netherlands**.
- **Return shipping costs:** The direct costs of returning the item are borne by you.

## 2. Inspection and diminished value

You may inspect the item as you would in a shop — look at it, try it on, examine it. If the item's value is diminished by handling beyond that (for example use, stains, or removal of attached tags/seals where applicable), we may deduct the diminished value from your refund, as permitted by law. We will always explain any deduction.

## 3. Refunds

Once we have received the returned item (or proof of return shipment), we refund within 14 days using your original payment method, including the original standard delivery cost where the full order is withdrawn, as required by law. Extra costs for a non-standard delivery option you chose are not refunded.

## 4. Exclusions

Only exclusions that are legally valid apply. For our assortment of pre-owned bags and accessories, the standard withdrawal right generally applies. Gift cards, if offered in future, may carry their own lawful conditions.

## 5. Faulty or not-as-described items (statutory rights)

Separate from the withdrawal right, statutory conformity rights apply. If your item is faulty or does not match its description, photos or condition report, contact us promptly. You are entitled to the remedies provided by law (such as repair, replacement where possible for a unique item, price reduction or refund). Disclosed and photographed wear on a pre-owned item is part of the agreed description and is not a defect. These statutory rights are never limited by this policy.

## 6. Authenticity concerns

If you have a concern about a piece you received, contact us directly; we take such concerns seriously and will respond personally.
