## Use of artificial intelligence

La Retrouvée is a forward-looking business. We use artificial intelligence (AI) where it improves your experience as a customer, always under human supervision. Among other things, AI supports us in:

- presenting our pieces, for example in editing product photos, writing product descriptions and translating them;
- parts of our authenticity procedure;
- drafting condition reports.

For us, AI is a tool, not the final word. Every piece is examined by us before it is listed, and we decide on authenticity and condition ourselves. In photos we use AI at most for the background, light or framing, never to alter the piece itself or to hide signs of wear. Product photos always show the piece you buy.
