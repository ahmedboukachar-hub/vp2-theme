# Content roadmap — Journal

Three tiers; build depth around stocked products, real customer questions
and processes we can explain from experience. Voice: a careful curator
showing another person what is worth noticing. See `docs/content-briefs.md`
for the older V1 briefs; this file is the authoritative roadmap.

## Status legend

- **draft** — exists as an unpublished Shopify article (EN + NL/DE/FR on
  the same resource). Publication requires explicit owner permission.
- **live** — published.
- **brief** — planned only.

not delete them before redirects exist.

## Rules

- Publication of any draft requires explicit owner permission.
- Every article: ≥2 of {close observation, decision framework, photographed
  detail, comparison table, wear note, caveat, internal guide+product link,
  cited sources}.
- Banned phrases per the redesign brief §22.1 (no "timeless elegance",
  "investment piece", "must-have", etc.). No authenticity claims.
