## Guardrails applied

- Not used as product images — product photos must show the actual piece
  being sold.
- No official designer campaign imagery or standalone brand logos; these are
  lifestyle/editorial photographs in which branded packaging or bags appear
  incidentally, consistent with a reseller of authentic pre-owned pieces.
- Alt texts are descriptive and make no authenticity or origin claims.
- Replace freely later via Theme Editor (homepage) and Admin (collections,
  blog posts) once brand photography exists; this document should be updated
  or retired at that point.
